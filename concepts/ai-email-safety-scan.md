AI Email Safety Scan — Concept Page

Status: To Do
Category: Email Security • AI Safety • UX Strategy
Created: August 2026
Summary

A user-facing AI feature that scans emails for phishing, fraud, and malicious intent.
The scan runs automatically when the user activates an AI action (like Summarize), providing a clear warning if the email appears unsafe.
Problem Statement

Users — especially mobile-only users — often interact with dangerous emails without realizing the risks.
Phishing messages use vague language, urgency, and fear tactics to manipulate people into clicking unsafe links or giving up credentials.
Core Idea

Integrate a visible AI Safety Scan into email providers’ existing AI tools.
When triggered, the scan evaluates:

    vague or manipulative language

    generic greetings

    urgency or fear-based threats

    mismatched sender domains

    unsafe links or redirects

    known phishing templates

    abnormal patterns compared to user behavior

If suspicious, the system displays a simple warning such as:
“This email appears unsafe. Be cautious before clicking links or responding.”
Why It Matters

    Protects mobile-only users

    Reduces device compromise

    Strengthens trust in email providers

    Reduces support burden

    Aligns with user-safety commitments

    Makes AI features feel protective, not just convenient
